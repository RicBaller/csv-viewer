View and edit CSV files online

CSV Viewer opens CSV and TSV files directly in your browser. View your data as a table, edit cells, rename or delete columns and rows, and download the result as a new CSV file. Free, no account, nothing to install.

  • Private Your file never leaves your device. Everything runs locally in your browser.
  • Edit Click a cell or column name to change it. Delete rows and columns, or select several rows and delete them at once. Add rows and columns at the end or anywhere in between.
  • Row view See one row at a time as column name and value. Handy for wide files.
  • Any delimiter Comma, semicolon, tab or pipe, with or without a header row.

Frequently asked questions

How do I open a CSV file?

Drag the file onto this page or click “Choose file”. Check the preview, pick the delimiter if needed and click “Load”.

Is my file uploaded?

No. Your file is read and processed only in your browser. No data is sent to a server.

How do I save my changes?

Click “Download CSV”. You get a new CSV file with all your changes, with the same delimiter as the original file.

Can I open CSV files from Excel?

Yes. The delimiter is detected automatically, also the semicolon that Excel often uses. The downloaded file opens correctly in Excel, special characters included.
